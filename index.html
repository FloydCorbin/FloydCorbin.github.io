About

Floyd is a Senior Solution Consultant at Genpact Experience, specializing in enterprise digital experience solutions built on Adobe Experience Manager (AEM) as a Cloud Service. He works with business and technical stakeholders to translate organizational objectives into practical platform strategies, bridging discovery, solution design, implementation planning, and operational readiness. His background in quality assurance, business analysis, software development, and project management brings a comprehensive perspective to digital transformation initiatives.

His Adobe expertise spans AEM Sites and Assets, Dynamic Media, Content Hub, enterprise Digital Asset Management (DAM), and integrations across the Adobe Experience Cloud ecosystem. His work includes DAM migration strategy, metadata and taxonomy architecture, content governance, requirements engineering, and content supply chain optimization. He has also evaluated emerging authoring approaches using Edge Delivery Services and Universal Editor, and supports organizations exploring Adobe Workfront integrations and more efficient content production and approval workflows.

Floyd has contributed to digital experience initiatives supporting organizations including OneBlood, Grand Design RV, Pluralsight, and Bungie Interactive. His consulting approach emphasizes cross-functional collaboration, thoughtful solution architecture, and sustainable operational processes that help organizations realize greater value from their technology investments.

Before transitioning into solution consulting, Floyd held quality assurance engineering positions at Rightpoint, Hoodoo Digital, and MRM/McCann. His experience included large-scale Sitecore migration testing for PhRMA.org and complex business logic validation for Panasonic's Toughbook Configurator. Throughout these engagements, he advocated for accessibility, structured testing, documentation, and early identification of delivery risks.

Earlier in his career, Floyd served as a Development Project Manager and Business Analyst for the State of Utah, contributing to initiatives involving the Prescription Drug Monitoring Program (PMP) Interconnect and interstate data exchange. He holds an MBA from Westminster College, a BS in Software Development from Western Governors University, and a BA in International Studies from the University of Utah.
