About

Floyd Corbin is an Adobe-certified Technical Strategist and Solution Consulting professional who helps organizations bridge business requirements with technical execution across enterprise CMS and DXP platforms. With a strong foundation in quality engineering and a broader focus on technical strategy, he partners with stakeholders to drive Requirements Discovery, shape solution recommendations, and manage cross-functional delivery from concept through launch. His work spans Solution Consulting, Technical Strategy, Requirements Discovery, and Cross-functional Stakeholder Management in complex digital transformation initiatives.

Floyd's expertise spans end-to-end project delivery across Adobe Experience Manager (AEMaaCS), Sitecore, and related CMS/DXP environments. During his tenure at Rightpoint, he has supported enterprise projects for organizations including Oneblood.org, Grand Design RV, Pluralsight, and Bungie Interactive, translating business goals into scalable digital experiences and measurable platform outcomes.

Prior to joining Rightpoint, Floyd contributed his skills at MRM/McCann, where he played a vital role in the PHRMA.org Sitecore 9 Program. This involved managing multiple projects, including a primary site with several micro-sites. Floyd also contributed to a project for the Panasonic Configurator, an application designed to configure the Toughbook line of computers, involving intensive business logic validation and delivery coordination.

Before his time at MRM/McCann, Floyd served as a Project Manager/Business Analyst for the State of Utah, working on various projects for the Prescription Drug Monitoring Program (PMP) Interconnect application. His responsibilities included leading a project to connect Utah's PMP to all other U.S. states.
